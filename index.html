About me

A lifelong passion
for animals

Hi there! I'm Sabrina, a devoted animal lover and lifelong caregiver. Originally from Argentina and currently based in the sunny south of Spain (Seville), I share my life with my partner Dani and our two beloved dogs. They aren't just pets; they are our family, so I deeply understand that your furry friends are the heart of your home.

Growing up, I learned that the bond between a person and their pet is something truly special, and I've dedicated time and effort to honour that bond every time I care for someone's beloved companion.

🐕 Experienced with dogs of all sizes & temperaments
🐈 Comfortable with cats and their independent nature
🐴 Volunteer experience with horses & farm animals
🏠 Treats every home and pet with the utmost respect
My Experience

Experience highlights

🐾
Diverse Dog Care

I've cared for various breeds and sizes, from spirited French Bulldogs and energetic Poodles to gentle Maltese mixes. I am experienced with all temperaments, from adventurous explorers to calm lap-dogs.

💙
Special Medical Needs

I have specific experience caring for pets with Ataxia. I am comfortable managing mobility challenges, assisting with movement, and following strict safety protocols.

🐴
Beyond Cats & Dogs

My volunteer work at Santuario La Candela, an equestrian centre, and an educational farm has given me hands-on experience with horses, goats, sheep, and farm animals.

🎓
Aspiring Veterinary Technician

I am currently training to become a Veterinary Technical Assistant, deepening my knowledge of animal health, clinical care, and medical support — because my love for animals goes far beyond companionship.

Why choose me
💻
Remote Worker

As a Web Developer and Spanish teacher, I work from home, providing 24/7 companionship and a consistent routine for your pets.

🌿
Respectful & Tidy

I treat every home with the utmost respect. As a plant lover, I'll also ensure your garden or indoor plants thrive while you're away.
