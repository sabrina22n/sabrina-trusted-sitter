About me

A lifelong passion
for animals

I'm Sabrina, and animals have always been the most important part of my life. Growing up, I learned that the bond between a person and their pet is something truly special — and I've dedicated time and effort to honour that bond every time I care for someone's beloved companion.

I offer calm, attentive, and personalised care — following each pet's existing routine so they feel safe and comfortable. Whether it's long walks, playtime, cuddle sessions, or just quiet company, I adapt to what each animal needs.

🐕 Experienced with dogs of all sizes & temperaments
🐈 Comfortable with cats and their independent nature
🐴 Volunteer experience with horses & farm animals
🏠 Treats every home and pet with the utmost respect
